● The problem

Money disappears in the gap between the budget and the ground.

Institutions — from government departments to small implementing organisations — track budgets on paper registers, spreadsheets, and disconnected systems. Nobody can see the full path a rupee takes.

01

No single source of truth

Budgets, approvals and spend records live in separate files, so reconciling who spent what takes weeks, not seconds.

02

Delayed payments, silent leakage

Without a live view of disbursement, funds sit idle at one tier while workers at the next tier wait — or worse, quietly vanish.

03

No real-time control

By the time an audit happens, the trail has gone cold. There's no way to catch an anomalous transaction while it still matters.

● The solution

One traceable hierarchy, from the top budget to the last worker.

A central admin creates a scheme and its budget. Members add the members below them — down to the contractors, institutions and workers actually spending it — so every rupee has a visible, verified path.

Admin

Creates the scheme, sets the initial budget, adds intermediate members.

→
Intermediate

Adds end users below them, verifies proof before it counts as spend.

→
End user

Teachers, contractors, service workers — upload proof of work and spend.

→
AI oversight

Anomalous transactions are flagged for review as they happen, not months later.

● Built for every tier

Every role gets exactly the view it needs.

Tier 01

Admin

Owns the scheme and the budget.

  • Create schemes with an initial budget and supporting PDF
  • Add intermediate members below them
  • Track ongoing and completed progress, per scheme
  • See flagged risk on any transaction
Tier 02

Intermediate

Manages end users and verifies proof.

  • Add end users below them
  • Review proof in "to be verified" before it posts
  • Verify progress and expenditure documents
  • Track ongoing and completed progress
Tier 03

End user

Teachers, contractors, service workers.

  • See their assigned project
  • Upload progress proof for their work
  • Upload expenditure proof for spend
  • Track their own verification status
